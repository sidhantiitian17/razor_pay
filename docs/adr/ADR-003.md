# ADR-003: Gross vs net money model and journal semantics

## Status
Accepted

## Context
PLAN.md did not distinguish gross and net amounts. Bank credits match net (gross - fee - tax), but this was unmodeled (D3, C3). Journals were not required to sum to zero.

## Decision
- `GatewayPayout.net_paise` = `amount_paise - fee_paise - tax_paise` (computed property)
- Bank credit matches `payout.net_paise`
- Ledger `settlements_receivable` matches `payout.amount_paise` (gross)
- Ledger `bank` line matches bank credit (net)
- Fee and tax lines account for the difference
- Every journal set sums to exactly 0 (I3), asserted at construction
- Money is `int` paise everywhere — no float (I1)
- Signed amounts: `settlements_receivable` is negative (clearing), `bank` is positive (cash received)

## Consequences
`fee_mismatch` bucket is now meaningful. The three-way match has precise semantics.
